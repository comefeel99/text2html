Sectoral analysis

By the end of 2025, manufacturing (IIoT), healthcare (diagnostic imaging, genomics), media entertainment (ultra-high-definition, immersive content), and professional services will be the largest data contributors. Consumer-generated content (CGC) continues to inject huge traffic through social platforms and short-form video applications.

More than 80% of data is unstructured, which is driving the rapid development of artificial intelligence (especially computer vision and natural language processing) and advanced analytics platforms. Businesses must invest in a technology stack that can understand, process, and extract insights from it.
