The Continual Expansion of the Dataverse

In 2025, the global datasphere—the total data created, captured, copied, and consumed—is projected to surpass 200 Zettabytes (ZB), a stark increase from approximately 50 ZB in 2020, highlighting exponential growth. The Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR) remains high at 25%-30%, driven by consumer interactions, enterprise digital transformation, and the widespread deployment of IoT devices.
