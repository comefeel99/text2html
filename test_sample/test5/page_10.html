Core Insights and Strategic Recommendations: Organization and Resilience

Based on Risk Data and Collaborative Efficacy Analysis, Proposing Action Plans to Strengthen Organizational Foundations and Respond to Uncertainty

Collaboration is a Growth Accelerator, but Deep-Seated Mechanisms and Data Barriers Persist

Cross-departmental project success highly depends on collaboration, but collaborative efficiency is constrained by process gaps and information silos. The focus for the next phase of organizational effectiveness improvement lies in breaking down the invisible “data walls” and “process walls” between departments, building a deep collaboration network based on shared goals and context.
