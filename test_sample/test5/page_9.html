Growth and Efficiency

Strategic Implication1

The marginal benefit of the model that simply pursues the growth of the number of customers has declined.

Data Support

Annual CAC increased by 15%, SMB churn rate increased by 1.5 percentage points, yet the LTV/CAC ratio for the high-value customer segment is as high as 12.5, and NRR is 108%.

Strategic Implication2

The future growth engine should focus more on the value of existing customers, especially high-value customers.
